About Me.

Kelechi Royal is a lover of God, singer-songwriter, and creative with a burning desire to make the reality of God's presence a living experience in our time. Through sound, words, and inspired artistic expressions, he heralds the move of God, creating music that awakens hearts and stirs spiritual consciousness. His desire is to translate heavenly inspiration into sound and expressions that allow listeners not only to encounter God's presence but to live from that experience. Beyond music, Kelechi is passionate about depicting the mind and ways of God through art and creativity. He is the founder of The Empire, an artistic movement devoted to revealing Jesus Christ through creative expression. If you desire to experience God through art, sound, and revelation, welcome home.
